Residential Care Plans

Every Care Plan keeps your home's equipment on a maintenance schedule, with preferred scheduling and member savings on repairs and services. The HVAC Care Plan covers two seasonal visits a year, spring and fall, to inspect and maintain your heating and cooling system, with replacement air filters included. The PLUMBING Care Plan covers one annual visit with a water heater flush, water pressure and water quality testing, and an inspection of your accessible plumbing.

If you'd like your plumbing and your HVAC system looked after together, the BUNDLED Care Plan brings the HVAC and PLUMBING services into one membership. At the top of the line, the PREMIER Care Plan includes all of that and adds priority scheduling, a reduced dispatch fee, after-hours savings, and complimentary water softener salt and RO filter replacement with your service visits. Both of these plans also include access to our Member Rewards Program.
