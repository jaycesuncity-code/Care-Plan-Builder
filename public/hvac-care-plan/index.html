Your air conditioner works hardest in July and your furnace works hardest in January — and both are far more likely to fail during peak demand than at any other time of year.

The HVAC Care Plan puts a Sun City technician in front of your equipment twice a year, so the small problems get caught on a scheduled visit instead of an emergency call.

What's Included in a Visit

Every visit follows the same thorough checklist, whether it's your first tune-up or your tenth. We check the components that fail most often and adjust anything running outside factory spec — the goal is to catch a problem while it's still an inspection, not a repair.

HVAC Visits

2× per year — spring & fall
  • Full system inspection & performance check
  • Clean coils and air-handling components
  • Clear condensate drain line
  • Inspect electrical connections & safety controls
  • Lubricate motor bearings and moving parts
  • Check refrigerant pressure and operating cycles
  • Test thermostat calibration
  • Tighten electrical connections & fasteners
