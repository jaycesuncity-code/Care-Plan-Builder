Sun City Loyalty Rewards Program

At Sun City Plumbing & Heating, we believe in rewarding our Rewards Members with real benefits that protect your home and save you money over time. Our Loyalty Rewards Program was created to provide long-term value, added peace of mind, and exclusive perks to customers who continue to trust our team year after year.

When you choose Sun City, you’re not just hiring a contractor for a one-time service call, you’re building a relationship with a company that is committed to helping you maintain your plumbing and HVAC systems for the long haul. From repairs and installations to future upgrades, our Loyalty Rewards members receive benefits designed to make home ownership easier, safer, and more affordable.

Our goal is simple: reward customers who stay proactive, stay protected, and stay with Sun City.

$100 Annual Loyalty Credit Toward Equipment Replacement

As a Loyalty Rewards member, you receive a $100 loyalty credit every year that can be applied toward the replacement of qualifying HVAC or plumbing equipment installed by Sun City Plumbing & Heating. This credit helps reduce the cost of major upgrades when the time comes, giving you a real advantage over customers who wait until a system fails unexpectedly.

This benefit was built specifically for homeowners who understand that equipment replacement is not a matter of if, but when. Heating and cooling systems, water heaters, and major plumbing components don’t last forever, and the customers who plan ahead are the ones who save the most money in the long run.

With our annual loyalty credit, you can build replacement savings year after year while continuing to rely on a company you already trust.

Program Details:

  • $100 credit earned per calendar year of active participation
  • Credits may accumulate for up to 25 consecutive years (maximum $2500 total credit)
  • Credits may only be applied toward full system replacement or major equipment replacement
  • Credit may be applied toward qualifying replacements such as HVAC systems, furnaces, air conditioners, water heaters, and other major installed equipment
  • Credits have no cash value, cannot be redeemed for refunds, and are non-transferable
  • Customer must remain in good standing at the time the credit is used
  • Credit must be applied at the time of purchase and cannot be retroactively applied to prior invoices

1-Year Labor Warranty on All Repairs & Equipment Installations

Loyalty Rewards members receive a full 1-year labor warranty on all repairs and equipment installations performed by Sun City Plumbing & Heating. This benefit adds an extra layer of protection beyond standard coverage and reflects our commitment to quality workmanship.

Many companies offer limited warranties, short labor coverage, or fine-print restrictions that leave customers paying out-of-pocket soon after service is completed. At Sun City, we believe that when you invest in professional service, you deserve confidence that the job was done correctly, and that we’ll stand behind it.

That’s why Loyalty Rewards members receive extended labor protection that covers workmanship-related labor issues for an entire year after service.

What This Means for You:

  • 12 months of labor coverage from the date of service
  • Applies to HVAC repairs such as furnaces, air conditioners, heat pumps, and airflow-related repairs
  • Applies to plumbing repairs including leaks, fixture repairs, water lines, and more
  • Applies to new equipment installations completed by our team
  • If a covered issue arises due to workmanship, labor costs are fully covered during the warranty period
  • Reduces the risk of unexpected labor expenses after major repairs or installations

This warranty provides peace of mind knowing that if something goes wrong related to the work performed, we take care of it — no hassle, no extra labor charges, and no back-and-forth.

(Manufacturer part warranties remain separate and are honored according to manufacturer terms and conditions.)

Annual Loyalty Giveaway & Raffle Access

Loyalty Rewards members receive exclusive access to our Annual Loyalty Giveaway, where active members are automatically entered into a raffle for premium prizes and home comfort upgrades. This event is our way of giving back to the customers who continue to support our company and refer friends and family year after year.

Our Loyalty Giveaway is more than just a promotion — it’s a tradition. Every year, we set aside high-value prizes designed to improve your home, increase comfort, and reward customers who stay connected with Sun City.

Members are entered automatically, and winners are selected fairly through a random drawing.

Prizes may include:

  • Smart thermostats
  • Water heaters
  • Air purification systems
  • Indoor air quality upgrades
  • Gift cards
  • Complimentary system services
  • Or other premium home comfort improvements

Winners are selected at random and announced publicly. There is no purchase required beyond active Loyalty Rewards membership to participate, and no additional steps are required to enter — if you’re a member, you’re in.

It’s just one more way we show appreciation to the customers who continue to choose Sun City Plumbing & Heating as their trusted home service provider.
